Post-Quantum Digital Signatures

December 2025 • Survey/Final

Abstract—The foundational security of modern Public-Key Infrastructure (PKI), reliant on algorithms like RSA and Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC), is critically threatened by the anticipated emergence of powerful quantum computers capable of executing Shor's algorithm. To ensure the long-term integrity and non-repudiation of digital communication, an urgent transition to Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) is required.

Index Terms—Post Quantum Cryptography, Digital Signatures, Hash-Based Signatures, R-LWE, Lattice, Public Key Infrastructure.
